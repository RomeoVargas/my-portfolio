Our process, from search to keys in hand

Every property journey starts with understanding what you really need. We listen, we research, we shortlist, and then we walk you through each home until one feels right.

  1. Discover
  2. Tour
  3. Offer
  4. Close

  1. Discover

    Step 1 · consultation

    We sit down, learn your priorities — neighbourhood, budget, commute, school district — and build a shortlist that fits.

  2. Tour

    Step 2 · viewings

    Private tours on your schedule. We point out what the listing won’t tell you: where the light lands, what the neighbours are like, the real noise level.

  3. Offer

    Step 3 · negotiation

    We handle the paperwork, negotiate on your behalf, and keep you informed at every turn until the deal is signed.

  4. Close

    Step 4 · keys in hand

    Final walkthrough, closing coordination, and then the best part — handing you the keys to your new home.

Questions buyers ask

The short answers. The long ones we give over coffee at a viewing.

How much does it cost to work with you?

For buyers, our services are free — the seller pays the commission. For sellers, our fee is competitive and transparent: we'll give you the number upfront.

How quickly can I view a property?

Usually within 48 hours. For new listings we often arrange same-day private viewings before the property goes public.

Do you help with financing?

We partner with trusted mortgage advisors who can pre-approve you quickly. We'll make the introduction — no obligation.

What neighbourhoods do you cover?

Oakwood, Harbor District, Elm Grove, Midtown, Riverside, and Birch Lane. Each of our agents lives in the area they cover.

How long does a typical sale take?

From first viewing to keys, most of our sales close in six to ten weeks. We keep every party moving and informed at each step.
