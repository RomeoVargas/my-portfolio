Four things we never compromise on

  • Locally sourced

    Every cut of meat and bunch of greens comes from farms within a hundred miles. The farmers know our name and we know their fields.

  • Wood-fired everything

    Our hearth burns oak and cherry wood all day long. No gas burners, no shortcuts. The smoke and the flame do the talking.

  • Seasonal menus

    The menu changes when the harvest does. If it's not at its peak, it's not on the plate. Come back in a month and dinner is different.

  • Handcrafted cocktails

    House-made syrups, fresh-squeezed citrus and spirits chosen one bottle at a time. Nothing premixed, nothing from a gun.

Common questions

Do I need a reservation?

Strongly recommended, especially Friday and Saturday. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar and we keep a few tables for them, but they go fast.

What's the dress code?

Smart casual. No flip-flops, no gym wear, but no need for a jacket. Come comfortable enough to enjoy your meal.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Absolutely. We have vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options on every menu. Let your server know about allergies and the kitchen will adapt.

Do you offer private dining?

Yes. The private dining room seats twenty with its own fireplace and a dedicated server. Use the form at the bottom of the page to enquire.

What's your corkage policy?

We charge $30 per bottle, limit two per table. Our sommelier is happy to suggest pairings from our own list instead.

Is there a kids menu?

Children are welcome. We offer a short kids menu with wood-fired pizza, grilled chicken and fresh pasta, all half-portions at friendly prices.

Where do I park?

There is a free lot behind the building with thirty spaces, plus street parking on Vine Lane. On busy nights we run a complimentary valet.
