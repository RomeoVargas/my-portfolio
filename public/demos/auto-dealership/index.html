03( Inspection )Before a vehicle reaches the showroom floor, our certified technicians spend half a day going through it bumper to bumper. What follows is a report, a price, and total confidence.

  1. 01( Mechanical )

    Engine, transmission, brakes, and suspension checked against factory specifications and logged.

  2. 02( Bodywork )

    Paint depth measured, panel gaps checked, and a full undercarriage rust inspection with photos.

  3. 03( Electronics )

    Every sensor, display, camera, and safety system tested. OBD-II codes cleared and verified.

  4. 04( Road test )

    A 20-mile road test covering highway, city, and hill driving. The report comes with the vehicle.

Before you visit

10( Questions )The six things people ask us first.

( Trade-in )Do you accept trade-ins?

Yes. Drive your current vehicle in and we will appraise it on the spot. The value comes straight off the purchase price.

( Finance )Can I get pre-approved online?

Yes. Fill out the form on our website and you will have a rate estimate within an hour. No hard pull until you decide to buy.

( Delivery )Do you deliver to my home?

Within 50 miles of the dealership, delivery is free. Beyond that we charge $1.50 per mile, or you can pick up from the lot.

( Warranty )What does the warranty cover?

Powertrain, electrical, and major components for 3 years or 36,000 miles. Extended coverage is available at purchase.

( Service )Do you have a service center?

A full service bay on site with factory-trained technicians. Oil changes, brakes, tires, and diagnostics — no appointment needed for quick jobs.

( Hours )When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00–19:00. Sunday 11:00–17:00. Test drives by appointment on Sundays.
