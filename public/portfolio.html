How I work

Four stages, one developer. From the first conversation to the last deploy, you work with the same person who understands your entire project.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We talk about your business, your users, and what success looks like. I map the requirements and propose the simplest path that gets you there.

  2. 2

    Design

    Wireframes, mockups, and a visual direction that fits your brand. You see every screen before a line of code is written.

  3. 3

    Build

    Clean, tested code shipped in short cycles. You get a staging link early and watch the project come together week by week.

  4. 4

    Launch

    Deploy, monitor, iterate. I stick around after launch to fix what real users surface and to help you grow what's working.
